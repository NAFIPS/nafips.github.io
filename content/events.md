Each year NAFIPS brings together the fuzzy sets and soft computing community for its
annual meeting — sometimes as a joint world congress with the International Fuzzy
Systems Association (IFSA) or alongside IEEE conferences. Related international
conferences the community follows include **FUZZ-IEEE** (IEEE World Congress on
Computational Intelligence), **IPMU**, and the **IFSA World Congress**.
