---

The following papers were recognized at **NAFIPS 2024**, held May 27–29, 2024 in
South Padre Island, Texas. Congratulations to all of the authors.

## Best Paper Award

- **Outlier Removal in Classification Datasets via Extracted Rules of a Deep Neuro-Fuzzy System** — Mojtaba Yeganejou, Scott Dick, and Michael Lipsett

## Outstanding Paper Awards

- **Adaptive Level Set Fuzzy Modeling** — Leandro Maciel, Rosangela Ballini, and Fernando Gomide
- **Equivalence between TSK Fuzzy Systems with Triangular Membership Functions and Neural Networks with ReLU Activation on the Real Line** — Barnabas Bede, Vladik Kreinovich, and Peter Toth
- **DCNFIS Regularization via Rule and Consequent Function Dropout Mechanisms** — Mojtaba Yeganejou, Scott Dick, and Michael Lipsett
- **Fuzzy Education: The Potential for an Agentic AI System to Advance Precision Medical Education using Large Language Models, Fuzzy Logic and Shapley Values** — Laurah Turner, Matthew Kelleher, Andrew Zahn, Eric Warm, David Furniss, Anoop Sathyan, Weibing Zheng, Seth Overla, and Kelly Cohen

## Best Student Paper Awards

- **Prediction Interval Fuzzy Model for Bath Temperature Estimation in an Electric Arc Furnace** — Aljaž Blažič, Igor Škrjanc, and Vito Logar
- **Optimizing Platoon Time Gap Following on the Highway Using Genetic Fuzzy Systems** — Heath Palmer, Kelly Cohen, and Anoop Sathyan

## Outstanding Student Paper Awards

- **Evaluating Language Models on Grooming Risk Estimation Using Fuzzy Theory** — Geetanjali Bihani, Tatiana Ringenberg, and Julia Rayz
- **A Model-Agnostic eXplainable AI–Based Fuzzy Framework for Sensor-Constrained Aerospace Maintenance Applications** — Bharadwaj Dogga, Anoop Sathyan, and Kelly Cohen

## Honorable Mention — Student Papers

- **Somewhat Surprisingly, (Subjective) Fuzzy Technique Can Help to Better Combine Measurement Results and Expert Estimates into a Model with Guaranteed Accuracy: Digital Twins and Beyond** — Niklas Winnewisser, Michael Beer, Olga Kosheleva, and Vladik Kreinovich
- **"Magic Mirror on the Wall, Who's the Fairest of Them All": Relying on LLMs for Accurate Information** — Yifei Hu, Tatiana Ringenberg, Tianyi Li, and Julia Rayz
