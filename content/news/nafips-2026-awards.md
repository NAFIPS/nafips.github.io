The Awards Committee recognized the following papers at **NAFIPS 2026**, held
March 14–16, 2026 in El Paso, Texas. Congratulations to all of the authors.

## Best Paper Award

- **A fuzzy logic framework for voxel-wise quality mapping in 4D Flow MRI** — Caroline Althouse, Julia Rayz, and Vitaliy Rayz

## Outstanding Paper Awards

- **Cost Minimization in LSP Medical Diagnostic Models** — Jozo Dujmovic
- **A SIR vaccine model on the interval context and control using Hamacher t-norm** — Marina Tuyako Mizukoshi, Masoud Asadi-Zeydabadi, and Weldon Lodwick
- **Quantum Rule-Based Fuzzy Systems in High Dimensionality Problems** — Prometeo Cortes Antonio, Fevrier Valdez, Patricia Melin, and Oscar Castillo
- **How to Make Fuzzy Estimates Less Subjective: Q-Sort, Intuitionistic Fuzzy, and Color Interpretation of Fuzzy Degrees** — Victor Timchenko, Yuriy Kondratenko, Christian Servin, Olga Kosheleva, and Vladik Kreinovich

## Best Student Paper Award

- **Fuzzy ODEs as a Means for Low-Thrust Trajectory Generation** — Nathan Steffen, Magnus Sieverding, and Kelly Cohen

## Outstanding Student Paper Awards

- **Modeling the Housing Deficit in the Sorocaba Metropolitan Region: A Fuzzy Inference System Approach** — Valdex de Jesus Santos, Joao Batista Neroni Jr., Hian Goncalvez dos Santos, Sandra Yukari Shirata Lancas, Sandra Regina M M Roveda, and Jose Arnaldo Roveda
- **Optimal Take-off under Fuzzy Clearances** — Hugo Henry, Arthur Tsai, and Kelly Cohen

## Honorable Mention — Student Papers

- **Fuzzy Feature Augmentation for Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning: Improving Model Robustness Under Noise Perturbation** — John Cavanaugh, Tri Nguyen, and Kelly Cohen
- **Bridging the Closed-World Gap: Interpretable Anomaly Detection via a Hybrid TSK-FCM Ensemble on the BETH Dataset** — Lohith Srikanth Pentapalli, Josette Riep, Justin Ouwerkerk, and Kelly Cohen
