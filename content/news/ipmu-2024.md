The **20th International Conference on Information Processing and Management of
Uncertainty in Knowledge-Based Systems (IPMU 2024)** takes place **July 22–26,
2024, in Lisbon, Portugal**.

IPMU is a long-running international forum for research on uncertainty modeling,
approximate reasoning, and knowledge-based systems.
