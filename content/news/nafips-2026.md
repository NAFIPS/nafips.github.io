**NAFIPS 2026** — the Society's annual conference on fuzzy systems and soft
computing — was hosted in **El Paso, Texas, USA**, on **March 14–16, 2026**,
with Christian Servin (El Paso Community College) among the local organizers.

The program covered fuzzy logic, fuzzy decision analysis, fuzzy control, fuzzy
neural networks, and related areas of soft computing.

Full details, including the program and proceedings information, are available on
the [conference website](https://sites.google.com/view/nafips26/home).
