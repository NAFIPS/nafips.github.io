The **2025 IFSA World Congress and NAFIPS Annual Meeting** — a joint congress of
the International Fuzzy Systems Association (IFSA) and NAFIPS — was held
**August 16–19, 2025, in Banff, Alberta, Canada**, with Marek Z. Reformat
(University of Alberta) serving as General Chair.

The congress brought together scientists, engineers, students, and practitioners
working in fuzzy logic and related areas to present recent research across the
theory and applications of fuzzy sets and soft computing.

## Plenary speakers

- **Sansanee Auephanwiriyakul** (Chiang Mai University) — *Fuzzy Pattern Recognition in Data Analysis*
- **Bernard De Baets** (Ghent University) — *Fuzzy Sets Turn Sixty: Time for a Health Check-up*
- **Marie-Jeanne Lesot** (Sorbonne Université, LIP6) — *Human-in-the-loop XAI: a Natural Playground for Fuzzy Logic*
- **Igor Škrjanc** (University of Ljubljana) — *Evolving Learning Frameworks for Adaptive Control and Monitoring in Different Application Areas*

Proceedings appeared in the Springer conference proceedings series.
