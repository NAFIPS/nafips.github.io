The **NAFIPS 2024 International Conference on Fuzzy Systems, Soft Computing, and
Explainable AI** takes place **May 27–29, 2024, in South Padre Island, Texas,
USA**.

The conference brings together researchers and practitioners to present the
latest advances in fuzzy systems, soft computing, and explainable artificial
intelligence.
