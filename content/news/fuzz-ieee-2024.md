**FUZZ-IEEE 2024** is held as part of the **IEEE World Congress on Computational
Intelligence (WCCI)**, taking place **June 30 – July 5, 2024, in Yokohama,
Japan**.

FUZZ-IEEE is one of the premier international conferences on fuzzy systems, and a
key event for the global fuzzy logic community.
