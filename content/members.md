## Become a member

NAFIPS is an **open society**. Anyone can become a member.

- **By attending.** Anyone who attends an annual NAFIPS meeting becomes a member of the Society.
- **By request.** If you would like to join between meetings, contact the [NAFIPS Secretary](/contact/) for details.

There are no membership dues to participate in the community — the Society is sustained through its annual conference.

## Join the mailing list

The **NAFIPS-L** mailing list keeps members informed about Society activities, conference announcements, awards, and news from the fuzzy sets and soft computing research community. It was created to facilitate communication among researchers in the field.

- **Subscribe or unsubscribe:** use the [NAFIPS-L subscription page](https://listserv.gsu.edu/cgi-bin/wa?SUBED1=NAFIPS-L&A=1).
- **Post to the list:** send email to `nafips-l@listserv.gsu.edu` once subscribed.

## Stay connected

The best way to become an active member of the community is to [attend the annual conference](/events/), present your work, and meet fellow researchers. Students are especially encouraged to take part — see the [Student Center](/students/) for awards and opportunities.
