Students and early-career researchers are at the heart of NAFIPS. Whether you are
presenting your first paper or leading a team in the competition, the annual
conference is the best place to share your work and meet the community.

## Student paper awards

At each annual conference, NAFIPS recognizes outstanding student research with
dedicated awards, including **Best Student Paper** and **Outstanding Student
Paper** awards, alongside honorable mentions. Papers are judged on originality,
technical quality, and contribution to the field.

Recent recognized student work has spanned prediction-interval fuzzy modeling,
genetic fuzzy systems for autonomous platooning, explainable AI for aerospace
maintenance, and the evaluation of language models using fuzzy theory. See the
[news archive](/news/) for the most recent award announcements.

## The fuzzy competition

NAFIPS conferences host a student **competition** that challenges teams to apply
fuzzy logic and soft computing to real-world problems. It is a great way to gain
hands-on experience, collaborate with peers, and have your work seen by leaders
in the field. Details are announced with each conference — watch the
[Events page](/events/) and the mailing list.

## Representation & mentorship

Students have a voice in how the Society is run: NAFIPS appoints **Special Young
Researcher Representatives** to its board, and the annual congress includes a
Student and Young Researchers chair. These roles help ensure the conference and
the Society serve the needs of the next generation of researchers.

## How to get involved

1. **Attend the conference.** Anyone who attends an annual NAFIPS meeting becomes
   a member — see [Events](/events/) for the next one.
2. **Submit your work.** Present a paper and you may be eligible for a student
   award.
3. **Join the mailing list.** Subscribe to [NAFIPS-L](/members/) for
   announcements, deadlines, and opportunities.
4. **Ask a question.** The [Student & Young Researcher representatives](/about/#board)
   are happy to help newcomers get started.
